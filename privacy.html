Privacy Policy

Sytu is a drawing practice archive app that helps you photograph studies, log practice notes, and generate monthly archive pages on your own device. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled inside the App.

1. Scope

This policy applies to the Sytu mobile application. The App is designed to work primarily as an on-device tool. It does not require creating a Sytu cloud account to use core practice logging features.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

Depending on how you use Sytu, the App may store the following locally on your device:

  • Practice entries you create, such as subject titles, practice dates, duration, paper size and type, medium, reference counts, mentor notes, self reviews, and tags
  • Photos you capture with the camera or import from your photo library for practice entries
  • Generated archive page outputs and related local indexes
  • Drafts that have not yet been finalized
  • Favorite or liked entry markers you set in the library
  • Drawing Credit balances and local purchase ledger records needed to avoid duplicate credit grants
  • Lightweight preference flags, such as whether onboarding or a permission prompt has already been shown

These materials remain in the App’s private storage on your device unless you export or save an image to your photo library yourself.

3. Information We Do Not Collect Through a Sytu Backend

Sytu does not operate a required sign-in service for this App surface. The App does not upload your drawings, notes, or drafts to a Sytu-operated backend as part of the core workflow described here. We do not ask you to submit government ID, home address, or other sensitive identity documents to use the App.

4. Permissions and Why They Are Requested

  • Camera — Requested only when you tap Take Photo so you can photograph a drawing for an entry.
  • Photo Library — Requested only when you choose an existing photo or save a finished archive page to Photos.
  • Microphone — Requested only when you tap Speak Notes.
  • Speech Recognition — Used only to convert Speak Notes audio into text for note fields you already have in the entry form.
  • Notifications — May be requested once after you reach the home library. In this surface the App does not run marketing push campaigns and does not assign a notification center delegate.

If you deny a permission, related actions may be unavailable, but other parts of the App can still work. You can change permissions later in iOS Settings.

5. Purchases

Optional consumable Drawing Credit packs are purchased through Apple’s StoreKit / App Store payment system. Apple processes payment details according to Apple’s own terms and privacy practices. Sytu stores only the minimum local transaction identifiers and credit ledger entries needed to grant credits correctly on this device. There is no Restore Purchases flow for these consumable credits.

6. Favorites and Local Preferences

If you tap the heart on a library card, that preference is stored locally so the App can show liked studies. Clearing app data removes those markers.

7. Data Retention, Export, and Deletion

  • You can delete individual practice entries from the entry detail screen.
  • You can delete generated archive outputs from the result screen.
  • Clear All Data removes entries, drafts, media files stored by the App, purchase ledger history, favorites, preference flags, and remaining credits for this installation.
  • Logging out of the welcome / Get Started screen does not by itself erase your local practice data; Clear All Data is the destructive wipe action.

8. Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13. Please do not use the App to store personal information about children.

9. Security

We rely on the device’s operating system protections for app sandbox storage. No method of electronic storage is perfectly secure. Protect your device passcode and backups accordingly.

10. Third-Party Services

Apple provides App Store distribution, StoreKit purchases, and optional system services such as speech recognition. Their handling of related data is governed by Apple’s policies. The App may open Privacy Policy and Terms pages in an in-app web view for your convenience.

11. International Users

Because content is stored on your device, your use of the App is subject to the laws that apply where you use the device and to Apple’s App Store rules in your region.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may revise this Privacy Policy as the App changes. The revised policy will be made available through the App’s legal links.

13. Contact

For privacy questions about Sytu, use the support channel listed on the App Store product page for this App.
